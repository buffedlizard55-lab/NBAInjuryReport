🔔 Alert center sound · browser notifications · manual-review links

Fires when a new listing appears or a status changes in any layer — the ESPN structured injury board, ESPN news, a recent allow-listed Bluesky post naming one recognized NBA player, or an explicit official report. In-game: when a monitored account posts exit or “questionable to return” language about a recognized player, an unconfirmed alert fires with the post link — that is the fastest signal a player hurt on a play is not coming back, and it does not wait for the official designation. ESPN's per-game DNP reasons are shown in the in-game monitor below but never sound, because a DNP row cannot prove an in-game exit. Every listing alert is tagged ⚡ HIGH LINEUP IMPACT when the player's collected box scores show a starter going out. DNP alone does not trigger an exit alert. Opening the page seeds existing items silently. Unresolved and ambiguous signals do not sound. Turn sound off (or mute a severity below) when you only want the log. The tab must remain open; background throttling and source delays can delay alerts.

Freshness has two meanings here and they are kept apart: a social alert must be from a post published in the last 30 minutes (so an old post resurfacing cannot re-alert), while a board/official alert fires when the change is observed — a listing's own source timestamp is legitimately hours older (ESPN stamps the report date; the NBA PDF is filed before tip). Suppression by source age is therefore applied only to the social layer.

The chime is a soft two-note bell (E5→A5, WebAudio — no file download). If your browser blocks autoplay until you interact with the page, press Test sound once; after that it plays on its own.

📡 Social layer verified accounts · free · no key

Reads the Bluesky/AT-Protocol public API for an allow-list of accounts whose identity evidence is recorded in the registry (full list on the Reporters page): the official NBA account, team accounts that carry a valid Bluesky verification object, and working reporters — a ✓ badge means the account's own Bluesky verification object was valid when checked, ◐ evidence means recorded evidence (curated-list membership / self-declared beat) which also qualifies a reporter for alerts. Posts containing injury language are classified and can raise alerts — including in-game exit language (“left the game”, “locker room”, “questionable to return”) before any official designation.
